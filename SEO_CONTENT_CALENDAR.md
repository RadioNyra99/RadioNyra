## Publishing Checklist

- One primary keyword per post
- Title under 60 characters where practical
- Meta description under 160 characters
- One H1 and logical H2/H3 structure
- Minimum 600 words for durable SEO posts; 300+ words for short news briefs
- Internal links to Listen Live, Schedule, FM Stations, related market pages, and Advertise
- Article or NewsArticle schema
- Original image or meaningful existing asset with alt text
